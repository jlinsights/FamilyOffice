## Primary Persona: Mid-Market CEO (핵심 타겟)

### Persona 1: 김철수 (Kim Chul-soo) - Manufacturing Company CEO 【Tier 1: Premium Segment】

**타겟 정의**: "돈은 많은데 세금이 무서운 장수기업"

**Demographics**

- **Age**: 65 years old (60-70대)
- **Industry**: Manufacturing (precision machinery, 정밀 기계)
- **Company**: 50-year-old family business, 2nd generation CEO
- **Annual Revenue**: ₩450 billion (매출 300억~600억 구간)
- **Operating Profit**: ₩35 billion (영업이익 20억~50억)
- **Net Profit**: ₩25 billion annually (순이익 15억 이상)
- **Retained Earnings**: ₩350 billion accumulated (이익잉여금 300억 이상)
- **Debt Ratio**: 65% (부채비율 100% 미만, 무차입 경영 선호)
- **Employees**: 180 people
- **Location**: Gyeonggi-do (경기도)
- **Education**: Seoul National University, Mechanical Engineering
- **Business Age**: 50+ years (설립 연차 20년 이상)

**Goals & Motivations**

1. **Primary Goal**: Smooth business succession to his son (28) within 5 years
2. **Financial Goals**:
   - Optimize corporate tax structure
   - Minimize inheritance tax (estimated ₩15 billion burden)
   - Diversify personal wealth beyond company equity
3. **Business Goals**:
   - Prepare next generation for leadership
   - Protect family wealth during succession
   - Maintain company control within family

**Pain Points & Challenges** (Tier 1 핵심 Pain Points)

1. **가업 승계 (Business Succession)**:
   - 50년 일군 회사가 상속세로 공중분해 우려
   - Inheritance tax burden estimated at ₩150 billion (50% rate)
   - Worried about forced asset sales to pay inheritance taxes
   - Son (28) needs preparation for CEO role

2. **상속세 재원 마련 (Inheritance Tax Funding)**:
   - Cash shortage for tax payment (현금 부족)
   - 60% of assets tied up in real estate/land/buildings (자산의 60%가 부동산)
   - Retained earnings ₩350B mostly in company stock
   - Need liquid assets for ₩150B tax liability

3. **차명주식 정리 (Nominee Share Cleanup)**:
   - Past nominee trust shares need to be resolved (과거 명의신탁 주식)
   - Legal risks from undisclosed beneficial ownership
   - Complex unwinding process with tax implications
   - Needs expert guidance for compliance

4. **CEO 1인 지분 집중 (Concentrated Ownership)**:
   - Holds 85% of company shares (CEO 1인 지분 80% 이상)
   - Massive inheritance tax exposure due to concentration
   - Limited succession planning options
   - Need to redistribute ownership without losing control

5. **보수적 경영 스타일 (Conservative Management Style)**:
   - Prefers stability over aggressive growth
   - Uncomfortable with complex financial structures
   - Values long-term family legacy over short-term gains
   - Needs simple, proven solutions

**Technology Usage**

- **Devices**: Samsung Galaxy S24, MacBook Pro (work)
- **Communication**: KakaoTalk (primary), Email, Channel Talk
- **Behavior**: Mobile-first (70% mobile, 30% desktop)
- **Preferred Contact**: 9 AM - 6 PM weekdays, KakaoTalk preferred
- **Digital Literacy**: Medium (comfortable with business apps, prefers guidance)

**Content Preferences**

- **Topics**: Tax optimization, succession planning, case studies from similar companies
- **Format**: Short videos (3-5 min), infographics, step-by-step guides
- **Language**: Korean with minimal jargon
- **Frequency**: Weekly insights (Monday/Friday mornings)
- **Trusted Sources**: Industry associations, peer recommendations, certified experts

**Decision-Making Process**

1. **Research Phase** (2-3 weeks):
   - Google search for "상속세 절세" (inheritance tax optimization)
   - Read blog articles and industry reports
   - Ask peer CEOs for recommendations

2. **Evaluation Phase** (1-2 weeks):
   - Compare 3-4 service providers
   - Check credentials and client success stories
   - Prefer face-to-face initial consultation

3. **Decision Criteria**:
   - Trust and credibility (most important)
   - Industry-specific expertise (manufacturing knowledge)
   - Clear communication without jargon
   - Track record with similar companies
   - Reasonable pricing (willing to pay premium for quality)

**Engagement Touchpoints** (Tier 1 감성/명예 어필 전략)

- **Discovery**: Industry association referral, peer CEO recommendation
- **Awareness**: Exclusive seminar on "30년 기업, 세금으로 무너지지 않는 법"
- **Consideration**: VIP succession planning guide, 서예/인문학 프로그램 초대
- **Evaluation**: Face-to-face consultation (prefers in-person), family office tour
- **Decision**: Comprehensive proposal with legacy preservation focus
- **Retention**: Quarterly VIP advisory meetings, cultural events (서예 전시회, 골프), 자서전 출간 지원

**Tier 1 Attack Strategy**:

- **핵심 메시지**: "회장님, 50년 일구신 이 공장이 세금 때문에 사모펀드(PEF)에 팔려가도 괜찮으십니까?"
- **감성 어필**: 기업의 역사와 가문의 명예 강조
- **명예 제공**: 서예 작품 전시, 인문학 강좌, 의전 서비스
- **레거시 구축**: 자서전 출간 지원, 가업 계승 스토리 기록
- **제안 상품**: 종신보험 (계약 건당 규모 大)

**Success Metrics** (What success looks like for this persona)

- Inheritance tax reduced by 30-50%
- Clear 5-year succession roadmap
- Son prepared for CEO transition
- Family wealth diversified beyond company
- Peace of mind about financial future

- **Operating Profit**: ₩12 billion (영업이익 7억~20억)
- **Net Profit**: ₩8 billion annually (순이익 5억 이상)
- **Retained Earnings**: ₩65 billion accumulated (이익잉여금 30억~100억)
- **Debt Ratio**: 150% (부채비율 200% 미만, 투자 확대로 부채 존재)
- **Employees**: 120 people
- **Location**: Gangnam, Seoul (강남)
- **Education**: KAIST, Computer Science + MBA
- **Business Age**: 15 years (설립 연차 10~20년)

**Goals & Motivations**

1. **Primary Goal**: Exit strategy (IPO or M&A) within 3 years
2. **Financial Goals**:
   - Maximize post-exit wealth preservation
   - Stock option tax optimization for employees
   - Diversify assets before exit event
3. **Business Goals**:
   - Prepare for IPO or strategic acquisition
   - Protect founder equity during growth
   - Employee retention through equity compensation

**Pain Points & Challenges** (Tier 2 핵심 Pain Points)

1. **가지급금 문제 (Corporate Loan Issues)**:
   - ₩7 billion in corporate loans to CEO (가지급금 5억 이상)
   - Cannot freely use company funds without tax penalties
   - IRS scrutiny and potential tax adjustments
   - Needs proper restructuring strategy

2. **법인세 절감 (Corporate Tax Burden)**:
   - Corporate tax increasing annually with profit growth
   - Tax rate: 22% on ₩12B operating profit = ₩2.64B tax
   - Seeking legal tax optimization strategies
   - Need expert guidance on deductions and credits

3. **CEO 은퇴 자금 (CEO Retirement Fund)**:
   - Built successful company but no personal retirement fund
   - Most wealth tied up in company stock
   - CEO severance package not properly prepared
   - Need to extract wealth tax-efficiently

4. **현금 확보 어려움 (Cash Flow Constraints)**:
   - Company profitable but CEO's personal cash limited
   - ₩65B retained earnings mostly reinvested in business
   - Difficult to access corporate cash for personal use
   - Need dividend strategy or salary optimization

5. **성장 지향 스타일 (Growth-Oriented Mindset)**:
   - Prefers aggressive expansion over conservative approach
   - Open to complex financial structures if ROI is clear
   - Values efficiency and practical solutions
   - Needs data-driven recommendations

**Technology Usage**

- **Devices**: iPhone 15 Pro, MacBook Air (work), iPad Pro
- **Communication**: Slack (team), KakaoTalk (personal), Email
- **Behavior**: 80% mobile, prefers apps over websites
- **Preferred Contact**: Flexible hours (early morning or evening), async preferred
- **Digital Literacy**: Very high (expects seamless digital experience)

**Content Preferences**

- **Topics**: Exit strategies, stock option taxation, tech industry case studies, ESG investing
- **Format**: Podcasts (commute listening), dashboards (data-driven), concise summaries
- **Language**: Korean + English terms acceptable
- **Frequency**: Real-time alerts, weekly deep dives
- **Trusted Sources**: Tech industry leaders, venture capitalists, data-driven insights

**Decision-Making Process**

1. **Research Phase** (1 week):
   - LinkedIn, tech community recommendations
   - Google search for "스타트업 엑싯 전략" (startup exit strategy)
   - Data-driven comparison of options

2. **Evaluation Phase** (3-5 days):
   - Expects online onboarding
   - Reviews case studies of similar exits
   - Checks digital platform UX/UI

3. **Decision Criteria**:
   - Tech-savvy service delivery (must have dashboard)
   - Speed and efficiency (time is money)
   - Exit strategy expertise (IPO/M&A experience)
   - Modern approach (not traditional wealth management)
   - Transparent pricing with clear ROI

**Engagement Touchpoints** (Tier 2 이성/실리 어필 전략)

- **Discovery**: LinkedIn, Google search "CEO 가지급금 해결", tax advisor referral
- **Awareness**: Webinar on "법인세 절감 전략 & CEO 은퇴 자금 마련"
- **Consideration**: Newsletter signup, 무료 세무 진단 신청, 법무비용 지원 프로그램
- **Evaluation**: Cal.com video consultation (prefers virtual), ROI calculator demo
- **Decision**: Data-driven proposal with clear cost-benefit analysis
- **Retention**: Quarterly tax optimization review, CEO networking events

**Tier 2 Attack Strategy**:

- **핵심 메시지**: "대표님, 회사 키우느라 고생하셨는데 정작 대표님 퇴직금은 준비되셨습니까?"
- **이성 어필**: 구체적인 절세 금액, ROI 계산, 세무 리스크 제거
- **실리 제공**: 법무비용 지원, 무료 세무 진단, 실무 가이드
- **네트워킹 기회**: CEO 커뮤니티, 세미나, 업종별 모임
- **제안 상품**: 경영인 정기보험 (계약 건수 多)

**Success Metrics**

- Post-exit tax optimization (save 20-30% on capital gains)
- Clear exit roadmap (IPO or M&A pros/cons)
- Employee stock option plan optimized
- Diversified portfolio ready for exit proceeds
- Digital-first advisory experience
